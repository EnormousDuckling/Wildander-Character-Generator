Wildlander Character Generator V1.0

by Enormous Duckling

This generator aims to randomize/narrow down your options while still leave you with some illusion of choice in the process.

During the character generation you will be prompted to choose a total of 6 Major, 6 Minor and 6 Banned skills.

You will also have a chance to roll variatey of different character/playthrough aspects avaliable to you during character creation.

This includes your Race, Standing Stone, Beggnings and your fate as Dragonborn.

In order to begin, proceed to "Roll the dice" section and follow instructions.

The section "Character Sheet" will provide you with a short summary of your choices.

Skill Tier explanation

Major Skills

These are the 6 skills you should choose when creating your character in Wildlanders custom background creator. They serve as you characters main skills. In order to increase the magnitude of your choices, it is suggested that you sepnd the inital 6 perk points to purchase the first perk of each Major Skill.

Minor Skills

Skills which are neither Major nor Banned. You can use these as you please, but you will not get the initial skill bonus from custom character creation. Use them within your own reason.

Banned Skills

Skills which are banned, will not show up in next steps of "The Choice Is Yours" as an option to make them Major. In order to increase the magnitude of your choices, it is suggested that you do not use these skills in your playthrough at all. Do not perform actions related to these skills, and/or do not level/invest any perks in it.
